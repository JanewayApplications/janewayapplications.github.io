Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

Curio is an app for keeping track of your book collection. It's published by Praxis Revenue Support Services LLC ("we," "us"). This policy explains what information Curio collects, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: we collect what we need to run the app, we never sell your information, and we don't show ads.

What we collect

Your account

Your email address and the display name you choose. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the name and email address that service shares with us. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email," we only see the private relay address Apple creates for you.

Your collection

Everything you add to Curio: books and their details (such as title, author, ISBN, edition, condition, signature, prices, value, shelf location, tags, and notes about where a book came from), your collections and wishlist, and any cover photos you upload. If you use the Lending Library when it arrives, we'll also store the names and contact details you enter for borrowers and the loans you record.

Your settings and ideas

Your preferences, like color theme and text size. If you send us an idea from Settings, we store what you wrote and which kind of device you sent it from.

Subscriptions

If you subscribe to Curio Plus, Apple or Google handles the payment. We never see your card or bank details. We receive your subscription status (for example, which plan you're on and when it renews) so we can unlock Plus features.

Camera and photos

Curio uses your camera only when you scan a barcode or photograph a cover, and your photo library only when you choose a picture. Barcodes are read on your device. A photo is uploaded only when you save it as a book's cover.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't show ads or share your information with advertisers.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How we use your information

To run Curio: signing you in, saving and syncing your collection across your devices, unlocking Plus features, answering your support requests, and improving the app based on the ideas you send.

Who helps us run Curio

We use a small number of service providers, who handle your information only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase stores your account and collection data and cover photos.
  • RevenueCat keeps track of subscription status.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in (if you choose them) and payments.
  • Expo delivers app updates to your device.

When you look up a book, Curio sends the ISBN or title you searched for to Open Library and Google Books to find its details and cover. No account information is sent with those lookups. Links to eBay and Amazon open those sites in your browser, where their own privacy policies apply.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users and the service.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information for as long as you have a Curio account. You can delete your account at any time in Settings > Delete account. That permanently removes your account, your collection, and your cover photos. You can also ask us to delete it by email. Copies in our providers' backups are erased as those backups expire.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Curio Plus subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.

Your choices

You can view and edit your information in the app at any time. To ask for a copy of your information, or for anything you can't do in the app, write to support@praxisrevenue.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your information, and we'll honor them.

Security

Your information is sent over encrypted connections, and each account can reach only its own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect what you trust us with.

Children

Curio isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll let you know in the app.
