Your collections. Simplified.

Curio is a home for the books you own and the ones you're still hunting for. Scan a barcode, and Curio fills in the rest.

What it does

  • Scan and goPoint your camera at a barcode, one book at a time or a whole shelf at once. Title, author, publisher, and cover come in on their own.
  • Every detail a collector cares aboutEdition, condition, signatures, what you paid, what it's worth, and where it came from.
  • Your shelves, spine outSee your library the way it stands at home, sorted by room and shelf, or switch to a searchable catalog.
  • Don't buy it twiceSearch before you buy and Curio tells you whether it's already on your shelf or your wishlist.
  • Share a bookSend a book's details to a friend by text or email in a tap.
