Delete your Curio account

You can delete your account and everything in it at any time, right from the app.

In the app

  1. Open Curio and tap Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm by tapping Delete account again.

Without the app

If you can't use the app, email support@praxisrevenue.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll confirm by email and delete it within 7 days.

What gets deleted

Your account, your sign-in details, every book and collection, your notes, your cover photos, your settings, and the ideas you've sent us. This can't be undone. Copies in our providers' backups are erased as those backups expire.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Curio Plus subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings so you aren't charged again.
